Badge specification
Two tiers, three channels
Colour alone cannot carry this — ten themes ship, four of them on a colour-blind-safe lightness ladder and four of them light. Each tier is separated three ways: a different icon shape, an outline versus a solid chip, and the elapsed hours printed as text.
52h Caution · 48h+
A clock, drawn in outline, in --caution. This is information, not an accusation — the slot has gone quiet, and the player is the first to know.
On hover
52h since last activity.
78h Warning · 72h+
A triangle, on a solid --alert chip. 72h is the same threshold as PROBLEM_STALE_HOURS, so on an In-Progress slot this badge means you are on the host's next report — and it appears before the report runs, which is the whole point.
On hover
78h since last activity. Please play this slot or report on your status.
Copy variants, not more tiers
Two things move the wording, and neither adds a badge. "Since last activity" is a lie on a slot that has never had any — when the clock runs from the room link, the sentence names that instead. And the actionable half is an instruction to the holder, so it appears only on your own slots; on someone else's it would be telling the wrong person what to do.

Badges appear on every slot a board draws — yours, the rest of your table, and any table opened through the peek. A stalled table is the room's problem, and seeing whose slot is holding it up is most of why you would open someone else's board.
Never badged
Three statuses are terminal for badging — the set FREE_COMPLETED_STATUSES, which already decides when a mission claim is released. A player who has done their part is never nagged. A slot with no timestamps at all is unknown, not stale, and stays quiet too.
Note the deliberate divergence: statusReport.ts keeps 100% in scope on purpose — a slot with every check but no goal still holds its world open — and its header says in as many words not to unify with this set. So a 100% slot can raise a lastPlayer warning on the host's report while showing no badge here. That is the intended trade: the badge is a nudge aimed at the player, not a mirror of the report.
100% Goaled Done — badge suppressed
Unstarted In-Progress — badge eligible
Unstarted slots carry no tracker timestamps of their own, so their clock runs from the room link instead — see the Data model board. They badge like anything else, on the same 48h/72h, which is what makes "you never started" visible on the player's own page rather than only on the host's report.
Theme check
--caution is the one colour this feature adds, and it needs a value per theme like every other token. --alert reuses the existing --neg, which is already tuned in all ten. Caution is orange, not amber, in every theme — amber is gold here, so an amber badge reads as decoration rather than a warning. Full derivation and all ten values are on the Theme values page.
